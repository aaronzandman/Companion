How personal and work time are worked out
    
        
  • Morning: from leaving home to the first Publix store or fuel stop is personal time and miles. If that trip is longer than 30 minutes, only the first 30 minutes are personal; the rest is work.
    • 
        
  • During the day: everything is work time and work miles.
    • 
        
  • Evening: from leaving the last Publix store to arriving home is personal. If that trip is longer than 30 minutes, the first part is work and only the last 30 minutes are personal.
    • 
        
  • Time at home before you leave and after you get back is off the clock.
    • 
        
  • Mark a stop as a fuel stop by tapping ⛽ on it in Today's list.
    • 
        
  • Miles go with the time they were driven in: miles driven during personal time are personal miles, miles driven during work time are work miles. They're measured, not shared out by time.
    • 
        
  • Miles come from the odometer when your adapter is connected (it's also read right at the 30-minute mark), otherwise from GPS distance (marked ≈).

Usage data

      
To see how many people use Companion, on what phones and in which areas, the app sends a small anonymous count when you open it (once a day) and when you use certain features. It's always on, and everything it sends is listed here.

      
What's sent
    
        
  • App and version, and which Route Planner you picked (for example )
    • 
        
  • What you used, by name only: opened, started or ended your day, emailed your time, sent calls to Route Planner, sent past calls to History, opened MPG trip logging, sent feedback
    • 
        
  • Your anonymous ID (below), once a day, so you're counted as one person across days and devices
    • 
        
  • Screen size, plus phone type and Android version (read from the app's built-in browser)
    • 
        
  • General area (country and state), worked out by the counting service from your internet address. The address itself isn't stored.
    • 
      

      
What's never sent
    
        
  • Your texts, dispatch calls, order numbers, or store numbers
    • 
        
  • Your GPS location, home location, routes, times, hours, mileage, or odometer readings
    • 
        
  • Your name, P number, email, or anything you type
    • 
        
  • Phone numbers, contacts, or any ID that identifies you or this phone
    • 
      

      
Where it goes
Counts go to GoatCounter, a privacy-focused counting service, the same one Route Planner uses. Like any website, it sees your internet address while receiving a count; it uses it only to work out country and state and to avoid double counting for a few hours, and never saves it. No personally identifiable information is collected. Only the app's owner sees the totals, and only as counts. Counts are only sent when you're online.

Set up the Ford connection

    
About 15 minutes, once. Companion then reads the van's odometer and oil life from Ford.

    
1. Link the van to your Ford account

    
    
      
  1. Install the Ford app (formerly FordPass) and sign in, or create an account.
    2. 
      
  2. Tap Add vehicle and enter the van's VIN (driver-side dashboard through the windshield, or the door-jamb sticker).
    3. 
      
  3. Start the van and accept the prompt on its center screen (often Allow access or Authorize).
    4. 
    

    
If the Ford app won't accept the VIN or the prompt never appears, the van may be controlled by the company fleet system. Ask the fleet team.

    
2. Create a Ford developer account

    
    
      
  1. Open developer.ford.com (North America).
    2. 
      
  2. Sign up, verify your email, and turn on two-step verification (Ford requires it).
    3. 
      
  3. Use only letters, numbers and spaces in your profile name.
    4. 
    

    
3. Register an application

    
    
      
  1. Create a new application. Name it something simple, like Companion (letters, numbers, spaces).
    2. 
      
  2. When asked how it's used, choose personal use.
    3. 
      
  3. Select all vehicle types and all available data categories.
    4. 
      
  4. For Redirect URIs, enter exactly:
    http://localhost:8080/ford/callback
    5. 
      
  5. Save. Ford shows a Client ID and a Client Secret. Copy the Secret right away; Ford shows it only once. Keep both private.
    6. 
      
  6. Wait up to 2 hours before connecting; Ford takes a while to switch new applications on.
    7. 
    

    
4. Connect Companion

    
    
      
  1. Here in Settings → Ford connection, enter the Client ID and Client Secret and tap Save Ford app details. They stay on this phone only.
    2. 
      
  2. Tap Connect Ford, sign in with your Ford account, choose the van, and allow access.
    3. 
      
  3. Tap Test Ford data. Check that the odometer matches the van's dash.
    4. 
      
  4. Leave Use Ford for the odometer on. Companion asks Ford for the odometer while you drive (when the adapter isn't connected), and shows oil life on the Timer tab.
    5. 
    

    
If something goes wrong

    
    
      
  • No vehicle to choose when connecting: the van isn't linked to that Ford account yet (step 1).
    • 
      
  • Error right after connecting: wait the 2 hours from step 3, then try again. Check the Redirect URI matches exactly.
    • 
      
  • "Wait a minute": Ford limits how often it can be asked. Try again shortly.
    • 
      
  • Odometer looks wrong: send feedback with a screenshot of Test Ford data and the dash.
    • 
      
  • Start over: tap Disconnect, then Connect Ford again.
    •
