Home location

Used to tell personal from work time and miles. Saved on this phone only and never sent anywhere.
How personal and work time are worked out
  • Morning: from leaving home to the first Publix store or fuel stop is personal time and miles. If that trip is longer than 30 minutes, only the first 30 minutes are personal; the rest is work.
  • During the day: everything is work time and work miles.
  • Evening: from leaving the last Publix store to arriving home is personal. If that trip is longer than 30 minutes, the first part is work and only the last 30 minutes are personal.
  • Time at home before you leave and after you get back is off the clock.
  • Mark a stop as a fuel stop by tapping ⛽ on it in Today's list.
  • Miles come from the odometer when your adapter is connected, otherwise from GPS (marked ≈).

Usage data

      
To see how many people use Companion, on what phones and in which areas, the app sends a small anonymous count when you open it (once a day) and when you use certain features. It's always on, and everything it sends is listed here.

Where it goes
Counts go to GoatCounter, a privacy-focused counting service, the same one Route Planner uses. Like any website, it sees your internet address while receiving a count; it uses it only to work out country and state and to avoid double counting for a few hours, and never saves it. No personally identifiable information is collected. Only the app's owner sees the totals, and only as counts. Counts are only sent when you're online.
